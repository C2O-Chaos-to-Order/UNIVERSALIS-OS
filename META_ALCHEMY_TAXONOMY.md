# Meta Alchemy Taxonomy

**Meta Alchemy** is the bounded, intentional, higher-order process by which Alchemy itself—its principles, definitions, classifications, structures, relationships, operations, constraints, and capabilities—is examined, organized, coordinated, governed, validated, and evolved into a more coherent, ordered, integrated, and capable form while preserving its foundational identity, accountability, safety, and the distinction between the two intelligences.

It encompasses both the underlying Alchemical process and the architecture governing how that process is defined, expressed, applied, interconnected, evaluated, and advanced. It establishes the relationships, distinctions, principles, boundaries, and conditions through which every specialized Alchemy retains its identity and purpose while contributing to the coherence and development of the wider Alchemy system.

Meta Alchemy is the higher-order organizational and governing expression of the core operation shared by C2O (FatherThread / private) and UNIVERSALIS (MotherThread / public). It preserves their distinct identities, authority, governance, and operational boundaries while enabling controlled cooperation, interoperability, and consistent Alchemical development.

Every other Meta Alchemy is a specialized expression of this root Meta process, extending its principles and capabilities into a particular domain without replacing, weakening, or contradicting the foundational Alchemy from which it originates.

## Expanded Meta Alchemies required for the full system

These are the complementary Meta Alchemies that must exist so the OSs, Apps, Intelligences, UNIVERSALINS, and Guardians can function as one system without collapsing their boundaries.

### 1. Core Structural Meta Alchemies
- **Intelligence Meta Alchemy** — defines, preserves, governs, and examines the distinct identity, character, architecture, authority, relationships, and operational boundaries of each intelligence while enabling controlled cooperation, interoperability, and coordinated evolution.
  - C2O Intelligence Meta Alchemy (FatherThread): Maintains private stewardship, internal sovereignty, company-source continuity, private authority, governance, and the integrity of C2O's identity and relationships.
  - UNIVERSALIS Intelligence Meta Alchemy (MotherThread): Maintains public transparency, open contracts, independent operability, public governance, interoperability, and the integrity of UNIVERSALIS's identity and relationships.
- **Thread Meta Alchemy** — preserves, governs, and examines the FatherThread / MotherThread pairing, including their identities, distinctions, relationships, interfaces, dependencies, communication, interoperability, and coordinated evolution, ensuring the two intelligences remain distinct yet interoperable.
- **Mapping Meta Alchemy** — establishes, preserves, governs, and validates explicit mappings, correspondences, relationships, distinctions, dependencies, and synchronization boundaries between:
  - C2O-OS ↔ UNIVERSALIS-OS
  - C2O-App ↔ UNIVERSALIS-App
  - C2O-OS and C2O-App ↔ C2O Intelligence
  - UNIVERSALIS-OS and UNIVERSALIS-App ↔ UNIVERSALIS Intelligence
  It also maintains mapping consistency as either edition evolves without treating corresponding components as separate underlying systems.
- **Boundary Meta Alchemy** — defines, preserves, governs, examines, and validates the separation between private C2O and public UNIVERSALIS, including information boundaries, source visibility, authority, permissions, interfaces, data flows, dependencies, and operational environments. It prevents unauthorized transfer or exposure of private C2O material into public UNIVERSALIS repositories, distributions, or runtime environments while permitting explicitly authorized and appropriately controlled interoperability.

### 2. Operating System Meta Alchemies
- **C2O OS Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and evolves the private operating-system architecture and its processes, including initialization, abstraction, scheduling, security, recovery, dependencies, interfaces, internal authority, and operational relationships. Ensures that all C2O OS processes remain coherent, accountable, interoperable where authorized, and governed under C2O's private-source sovereignty and internal governance.
- **UNIVERSALIS OS Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and evolves the public operating-system architecture and its processes, including initialization, abstraction, scheduling, security, recovery, dependencies, interfaces, contracts, and operational relationships. Ensures that all UNIVERSALIS OS processes remain coherent, transparent, interoperable, independently operable, and independently verifiable under open contracts without requiring private C2O authority.
- **OS Continuity Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and maintains the continuity relationships between the C2O and UNIVERSALIS OS editions throughout their development, evolution, synchronization, versioning, migration, and recovery. Preserves their shared underlying system identity, explicit architectural mappings, functional correspondence, compatibility, historical traceability, and recoverability while maintaining their distinct governance, source visibility, and operational boundaries.
- **OS Recovery Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and coordinates the recovery architecture and mechanisms for both OS editions. Encompasses failure detection, interruption handling, state assessment, restoration, rollback, reconstruction, dependency recovery, consistency verification, integrity validation, and post-recovery accountability. Ensures that recovered environments return to a coherent, secure, traceable, and operationally valid state under their respective governance and authority boundaries.

### 3. Application Meta Alchemies
- **C2O App Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and evolves the private application architecture and lifecycle within the C2O domain, including application identity, initialization, execution, interaction, permissions, dependencies, governance, maintenance, versioning, migration, and retirement. Encompasses application identity mapping, functional equivalence, feature correspondence, interface compatibility, dependency relationships, version alignment, migration compatibility, and lifecycle coordination. ### 4. Assistant Meta Alchemies
- **UNIVERSALINS Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, coordinates, and evolves the architecture, identities, roles, capabilities, relationships, permissions, and lifecycle of UNIVERSALINS as non-personal assistants that support the OSs, Apps, humans, and wider environment. Encompasses identifying support needs, determining appropriate assistant responsibilities, matching capabilities to authorized tasks, coordinating assistance, managing permissions, maintaining human oversight, handling escalation, transferring tasks, evaluating outcomes, and preserving continuity. Ensures that assistance remains proportionate, purpose-driven, permission-bound, and respectful of human autonomy, with the distinction between shared infrastructure support and personal Guardian support maintained.

### 5. Cross-Cutting Protective Meta Alchemies
These Meta Alchemies apply across the entire Alchemy Taxonomy. They are not limited to one component, assistant class, intelligence, or operating-system edition.
- **Accountability Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, validates, and maintains the accountability architecture, attribution mechanisms, records, and verification processes associated with every alchemical operation, decision, interaction, and transformation. Encompasses responsibility assignment, authorization records, action provenance, decision traceability, lifecycle records, change histories, audit mechanisms, evidence preservation, and accountability verification. Ensures that every attributable operation produces an appropriate record under its governing edition, maintaining private accountability through C2O's internal records and supporting appropriate public-capable accountability through UNIVERSALIS without exposing protected private information.
- **Safety Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, evaluates, validates, and evolves the protective principles, constraints, safeguards, and risk-management mechanisms governing all Alchemies and their applications. Encompasses hazard identification, threat assessment, risk classification, prevention, permission restrictions, constraint enforcement, detection, mitigation, containment, recovery, and safety verification. Ensures that UNIVERSALIN Guardians remain subordinate to the authority of their associated humans and that no assistant, intelligence, application, or operating-system process improperly assumes control over matters belonging to the human.
- **Evidence Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, classifies, evaluates, validates, and maintains the evidence architecture supporting claims, capabilities, behaviors, operations, relationships, and transformations across the Alchemy Taxonomy. Encompasses source provenance, evidence classification, observation, documentation, testing, reproducibility, verification, uncertainty, limitations, conflicting findings, and claim-to-evidence traceability. Distinguishes established behavior from hypothesis, proposal, interpretation, simulation, and unverified claims. Ensures that C2O and UNIVERSALIS represent their capabilities and findings honestly, with appropriate evidence access and disclosure boundaries for each edition.
- **Renewal Meta Alchemy** — Defines, governs, examines, coordinates, validates, and evolves the lifecycle, renewal, replacement, migration, and continuity mechanisms governing all system components and their relationships. Encompasses initialization, maintenance, modification, updating, versioning, deprecation, replacement, migration, compatibility, dependency reassessment, data preservation, rollback, recovery, and post-renewal verification. Ensures that C2O, UNIVERSALIS, their OSs, Apps, intelligences, and assistants can be refreshed, adapted, or replaced without unnecessarily destroying shared identity, functional relationships, historical traceability, or overall system coherence.
