# Meta Alchemy Taxonomy

**Meta Alchemy** is the bounded, intentional, higher-order process through which Alchemy itself—its principles, definitions, classifications, structures, relationships, operations, constraints, and capabilities—is defined, examined, organized, governed, coordinated, validated, maintained, and evolved into a more ordered, coherent, integrated, and capable form while preserving its foundational transformative purpose, accountability, safety, and the distinction between the two intelligences.

It encompasses both the underlying Alchemical process and the architecture governing how that process and its specialized expressions are identified, understood, classified, organized, interconnected, applied, evaluated, and advanced. It establishes the principles, relationships, distinctions, boundaries, and conditions through which every specialized Meta Alchemy retains its identity and purpose while contributing to the coherence and development of the wider Alchemy system.

Meta Alchemy is the higher-order organizational and governing expression of the core operation shared by C2O (FatherThread / private) and UNIVERSALIS (MotherThread / public). It preserves their distinct identities, authority, governance, source visibility, and operational boundaries while enabling controlled cooperation, interoperability, and consistent Alchemical development. Every specialized Meta Alchemy is an expression of this root Meta process, extending its capabilities into a particular domain without replacing, weakening, or contradicting the foundational Alchemy from which it originates.

## Foundational Principles of the Meta Alchemy Taxonomy

The following principles govern the entire Meta Alchemy Taxonomy:

1. **Foundational Continuity** — every Meta Alchemy retains the identity, purpose, and foundational responsibilities of its corresponding Basic Alchemy while adding higher-order capabilities.
2. **Classification Integrity** — every Alchemy retains an identifiable classification, definition, scope, and place within the wider taxonomy.
3. **Relationship Integrity** — relationships, dependencies, distinctions, mappings, intersections, and combinations between Alchemies remain explicit and traceable.
4. **Architectural Coherence** — specialized Alchemies contribute to one coherent system without losing their individual identities or responsibilities.
5. **Edition Separation** — C2O and UNIVERSALIS retain their respective governance, authority, source visibility, and operational boundaries.
6. **Assistant and Intelligence Distinction** — C2O and UNIVERSALIS remain the two core intelligences; UNIVERSALINS remain non-personal assistants, and UNIVERSALIN Guardians remain personal human assistants and guardians.
7. **Human Sovereignty** — personal Guardians, personal information, and capabilities concerning a human remain subject to that human's consent, autonomy, and final authority.
8. **Accountability and Evidence** — Alchemical operations, transformations, and claims remain attributable, traceable, and appropriately supported by evidence.
9. **Safety and Boundaries** — every Alchemy operates within defined constraints, permissions, responsibilities, and authority boundaries.
10. **Controlled Evolution** — the taxonomy and its specialized Alchemies may expand, reorganize, and evolve without unnecessarily destroying foundational meaning, valid classifications, or established relationships.

## Relationship to the Basic Alchemy Taxonomy

The Meta Alchemy Taxonomy retains the complete foundational structure and responsibilities of the Basic Alchemy Taxonomy.

It does not replace the original Alchemies, discard their classifications, or turn their domain responsibilities into abstract descriptions. Instead, it upgrades each corresponding Alchemy with higher-order capabilities for definition, classification, architectural organization, relationship governance, examination, validation, coordination, accountability, and evolution.

**Basic Alchemy** defines and performs the bounded transformation of states, relationships, structures, and capabilities.

**Meta Alchemy** preserves that transformative operation while also defining, examining, organizing, governing, validating, and evolving the complete framework through which Alchemy and its specialized expressions are understood, related, applied, and advanced.

Every Meta Alchemy therefore remains both a continuation of its corresponding Basic Alchemy and a specialized expression of the root Meta Alchemy.

10. **Safety and Existential Integrity** — existential transformations account for identity risks, meaning distortion, dignity protections, lifecycle boundaries, and preservation of human authority.
