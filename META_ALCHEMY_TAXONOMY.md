# Meta Alchemy Taxonomy

**Meta Alchemy** is the bounded, intentional process by which a state, relationship, structure, or capability is transformed into a more ordered, coherent, or elevated form while preserving accountability, safety, and the distinction between the two intelligences.

It is the core operation shared by C2O (FatherThread / private) and UNIVERSALIS (MotherThread / public). Every other Meta Alchemy is a specialized expression of this root process.

## Expanded Meta Alchemies required for the full system

These are the complementary Meta Alchemies that must exist so the OSs, Apps, Intelligences, UNIVERSALINS, and Guardians can function as one system without collapsing their boundaries.

### 1. Core Structural Meta Alchemies
- **Intelligence Meta Alchemy** — defines and maintains the distinct character of each intelligence while allowing controlled cooperation.
  - C2O Intelligence Meta Alchemy (FatherThread): private stewardship, internal sovereignty, company-source continuity.
  - UNIVERSALIS Intelligence Meta Alchemy (MotherThread): public transparency, open contracts, independent operability.
- **Thread Meta Alchemy** — preserves the FatherThread / MotherThread pairing so the two intelligences remain distinct yet interoperable.
- **Mapping Meta Alchemy** — maintains the explicit mappings between C2O-OS ↔ UNIVERSALIS-OS, C2O-App ↔ UNIVERSALIS-App, and both to their respective intelligences.
- **Boundary Meta Alchemy** — enforces the hard separation between private C2O and public UNIVERSALIS so no private material crosses into public repositories or runtime.

### 2. Operating System Meta Alchemies
- **C2O OS Meta Alchemy** — private operating-system processes that initialize, abstract, schedule, secure, and recover the C2O environment under internal governance.
- **UNIVERSALIS OS Meta Alchemy** — public operating-system processes that do the same under open contracts, with transparent interfaces and independent verifiability.
- **OS Continuity Meta Alchemy** — ensures both OS editions can evolve while remaining mapped and recoverable.
- **OS Recovery Meta Alchemy** — restores a consistent, accountable state after failure or interruption for either OS.
