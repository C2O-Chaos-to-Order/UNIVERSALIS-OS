## 2. Method and decision rule

Each pair is assessed for evidence of a specific, defensible interface between the two canonical subject scopes. A narrow clinical, laboratory, or public-health application is not treated as proof of equivalence, containment, or universal dependency.

The review distinguishes:
- a relationship between separate canonical subjects from shared identity or equivalence;
- bounded clinical, laboratory, or population-health interfaces from whole-subject relationships;
- a knowledge or evidence contribution from operational dependency or enablement;
- evidence quality from owner acceptance and canonical registration.

A candidate RD-P recommendation is a proposal for owner review, not an accepted or registered relationship. Direction is recorded only where the sources support a meaningful contribution or information flow. Any reciprocal description is limited to the documented interface.
