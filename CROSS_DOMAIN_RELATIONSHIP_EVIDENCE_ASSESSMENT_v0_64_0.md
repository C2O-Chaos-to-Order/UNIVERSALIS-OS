A non-promotion in this report does not deny the scientific relevance of Biology to the health subject. It means that the full Biology ↔ target-subject pair has not been shown to add a separately bounded relationship to the programme's existing records. New evidence could support future reassessment if it identifies a method, decision boundary, or output that is genuinely additional.

## 4. Duplicate, distinction, and scope conclusions

The three selected pairs are new exact unordered pairs in the assessment ledger, but their scientific content substantially overlaps broader integrated relationships that have already been assessed:

- **Biology ↔ Epidemiology** is a real interface, particularly through molecular and genomic epidemiology. The additional Biology-root relationship is not independently bounded beyond LSHS-235 and existing specialist epidemiology relationships.
- **Biology ↔ Nutrition** is a real interface through nutritional biology and precision-nutrition mechanisms. The additional Biology-root relationship is not independently bounded beyond LSHS-236 and the existing specialist nutrition relationships.
- **Biology ↔ Physical Therapy** is a real interface through exercise adaptation, tissue responses, and neuroplasticity. The additional Biology-root relationship is not independently bounded beyond LSHS-237 and the existing specialist physical-therapy relationships.

These decisions do not imply that Biology is irrelevant to any of the three disciplines. They preserve a distinction between a genuine scientific interface and a justified additional relationship record. They also avoid interpreting an existing broader Life Sciences relationship as an exact-pair duplicate: the new pairs were screened as unique exact pairs, then not promoted after the incremental-scope test.

No assessment in this subgroup creates a hierarchy edge, universal dependency, or enablement claim. No new candidate RD-P recommendation is created.
