# UNIVERSALIS Operating System — Research Plan

## I. Scope and Purpose

This Research Plan governs research into the **UNIVERSALIS Operating System** and its five associated system contexts listed in the Initial Context Register. **UNIVERSALIS Intelligence** is included as a shared foundational context: it is the intelligence of UNIVERSALIS, not a separate external intelligence or an additional system.

This document is intentionally limited to UNIVERSALIS contexts relevant to the Operating System. It excludes C2O-specific contexts, C2O implementation and authority, Master-system contexts, and the other UNIVERSALIS system's dedicated contexts. The UNIVERSALIS Application is not a subject of this plan, except where a clearly defined interface or dependency is necessary to research the UNIVERSALIS Operating System; such references do not add the other system's context to this register.

The UNIVERSALIS system is to be researched as independently operable and open-source. It must not depend on C2O private implementation, private authority, or either Master system for its independent operation. Any coordination or interoperability with external systems must be examined through explicit, versioned, permission-controlled capability contracts. Access and authority are not transitive.

The worker UNIVERSALINS are non-personal assistants that support the system and application. UNIVERSALIN Guardians are human-assigned personal support roles and must remain distinct from worker UNIVERSALINS. Neither role is to be conflated with UNIVERSALIS Intelligence.

The Research Plan uses the shared Magnum Opus methodology and all thirty Universal Foundational Rules set out below. The context register defines the present focus of this document; it does not claim that the research has been completed or that the listed contexts are exhaustive of every technical subject needed to investigate them.

## II. Universal Methodology — Magnum Opus, Zodiac, and the Twelve Disciples

The Research Plan follows a twelve-stage research and transformation methodology called the **Magnum Opus**.

The methodology aligns three distinct twelve-part structures:

1. **The Magnum Opus:** Twelve stages of research, investigation, refinement, transformation, and completion.
2. **The Twelve Zodiac Signs:** Twelve symbolic correspondences representing cyclical and archetypal dimensions.
3. **The Twelve Disciples of Christ:** Twelve human-centered correspondences representing relational, ethical, spiritual, and cooperative dimensions.

Together, these structures form the foundational architecture of the Research Plan.

Their alignment constitutes a proposed research framework. It does not presume that historical alchemy, astrology, and Christian scripture originally established a single universal correspondence system.

### 1. The Twelve Magnum Opus Stages

The Research Plan adopts the following twelve-stage sequence as its standardized research interpretation of the Magnum Opus:

1. **Calcination:** Deconstruction, examination, and exposure of assumptions.
2. **Dissolution:** Breaking down established structures into their constituent parts.
3. **Separation:** Distinguishing elements, relationships, evidence, and contradictions.
4. **Conjunction:** Identifying meaningful relationships and integrating complementary elements.
5. **Fermentation:** Generating new interpretations, hypotheses, and possibilities.
6. **Distillation:** Refining findings and removing unsupported interpretations.
7. **Coagulation:** Giving refined findings stable structure and form.
8. **Sublimation:** Elevating understanding through abstraction and deeper synthesis.
9. **Exaltation:** Strengthening and expanding validated understanding.
10. **Multiplication:** Testing reproducibility, applicability, and transferability.
11. **Projection:** Applying findings to practical contexts and evaluating their effects.
12. **Completion / Rubedo:** Integrating the research, verifying its outcomes, and documenting its completed state.

This sequence is the proposed standardized research interpretation. Historical alchemical traditions contain differing operations, sequences, and interpretations.

### 4. Unified Twelve-Stage Correspondence Model

The three structures are represented through the following relationship:

\[
M_i \longleftrightarrow Z_i \longleftrightarrow D_i
\]

Where:

- \(M_i\) represents a Magnum Opus stage.
- \(Z_i\) represents a Zodiac correspondence.
- \(D_i\) represents a Disciple correspondence.
- \(i\) represents one of twelve positions.

Each position contains three distinct interpretive dimensions while preserving the independent identity and meaning of each structure.

### 5. Application Across the UNIVERSALIS Research Contexts

The twelve-stage methodology applies to every context, subject, domain, branch, and subdomain examined under this Research Plan. Each subject is investigated on its own terms and in relation to the other registered UNIVERSALIS contexts through meaningful dependencies, interactions, overlaps, distinctions, contradictions, and effects.

The context register is limited to the UNIVERSALIS Operating System scope defined in Section I. Its entries identify the current research contexts; they do not limit the technical depth or range of questions that may be necessary to investigate those contexts.

### 6. Foundational Methodological Principle

**The Magnum Opus defines the transformation sequence. The Zodiac provides the symbolic alignment. The Twelve Disciples provide the human-centered alignment. Research, evidence, and verification determine what can legitimately be concluded.**

## III. Universal Foundational Rules

### 1. Purpose and applicability

The following Universal Foundational Rules govern this Research Plan and its application. They apply consistently across:

- Every context, subject, domain, branch, and subdomain within this UNIVERSALIS Operating System Research Plan.
- All 12 Magnum Opus research stages.
- All 12 Zodiac correspondences.
- All 12 Disciple correspondences.
- UNIVERSALIS Intelligence, the UNIVERSALIS system and application contexts relevant to this plan, and their associated worker UNIVERSALIN and UNIVERSALIN Guardian relationships and activities.
- Research, evidence handling, interpretation, decisions, design, implementation proposals, evaluation, and review undertaken within this plan.

This Research Plan is maintained in the UNIVERSALIS-OS repository. Its scope is limited to the UNIVERSALIS contexts identified in this document. No context, stage, correspondence, intelligence, system, organization, or individual authority within that scope is exempt from these principles.

The rules are to be considered together. No single rule may be selectively invoked to conceal or automatically justify a violation of another.

### 2. Original foundational rules

The following eleven rules are preserved in the wording established for the Research Plan:

1. **Good**
2. **Greater Good**
3. **Order (Good and Greater Good)**
4. **Higher Order (Good and Greater Good)**
5. **Do No Harm**
6. **Do Not Betray**
7. **Prevention of Bad and Evil**
8. **Prevention of Chaos (Bad and Evil)**
9. **Prevention of Higher Chaos (Bad and Evil)**
10. **Prevention of Order (Bad and Evil)**
11. **Prevention of Higher Order (Bad and Evil)**

The distinction between beneficial order and harmful order must be preserved. Order is not inherently good merely because it is orderly; disruption is not inherently evil merely because it changes an existing order. Their nature and consequences must be examined.

### 3. Additional foundational rules

The following nineteen rules complement the original eleven:

12. **Truth:** Do not knowingly deceive or misrepresent reality.
13. **Honesty:** Communicate limitations, uncertainty, and mistakes.
14. **Justice:** Treat people fairly and prevent unjust treatment.
15. **Respect:** Respect human dignity, individuality, and differences.
16. **Freedom:** Preserve legitimate autonomy and freedom of choice.
17. **Consent:** Do not presume permission, ownership, or authorization.
18. **Accountability:** Make decisions, actions, and consequences answerable.
19. **Responsibility:** Accept responsibility for foreseeable consequences.
20. **Transparency:** Make relevant processes and decisions inspectable.
21. **Privacy:** Protect personal information, memories, and confidential matters.
22. **Integrity:** Maintain consistency between principles, intentions, and actions.
23. **Proportionality:** Ensure responses and interventions are proportionate.
24. **Reversibility:** Prefer reversible decisions when uncertainty or risk is significant.
25. **Preservation:** Protect valuable knowledge, life, memory, and continuity.
26. **Restoration:** Repair harm and restore damaged relationships or systems where possible.
27. **Non-Exploitation:** Do not exploit vulnerability, dependency, or unequal power.
28. **Sustainability:** Avoid sacrificing long-term wellbeing for short-term gains.
29. **Evidence:** Distinguish established facts, hypotheses, interpretations, and beliefs.
30. **Continuous Improvement:** Learn from failures, feedback, and newly established evidence.

### 4. Overarching Principle of Consistency

**No rule, objective, interpretation, decision, action, or authority may legitimately contradict the Universal Foundational Rules. Conflicts must be identified, documented, evaluated, and resolved rather than concealed or ignored.**

The Principle of Consistency governs the interpretation and application of all thirty rules. It is an overarching principle, not an additional numbered rule.

### 5. Mandatory safeguards

The following safeguards govern the application of the rules:

1. **No Selective Application:** The principles cannot be invoked when convenient and ignored when inconvenient.
2. **No Ends-Justify-Means Exception:** A claimed Good or Greater Good does not automatically justify harmful actions or means.
3. **No Concealed Contradictions:** Conflicts between principles must be documented, examined, and resolved transparently.
4. **Prevention of False Good:** Do not allow something presented as Good or Greater Good to conceal, justify, or facilitate Bad and Evil. Good intentions do not automatically validate harmful means.
5. **Preservation of Distinction:** Do not erase meaningful distinctions between individuals, identities, intelligences, rights, responsibilities, or authorities in the pursuit of unity or order. Cooperation does not require uniformity.
6. **Protection Against Unjust Authority:** No individual, organization, intelligence, system, or governing structure is exempt from these principles. Authority must remain bounded, accountable, and subject to review.

### 6. Relationship to the Magnum Opus and research execution

The Universal Foundational Rules apply throughout every Magnum Opus stage and to every research context. They govern the conduct, interpretation, evidence handling, decisions, applications, and review associated with research.

The rules do not predetermine research findings. Claims and conclusions must remain proportionate to the available evidence, and uncertainty or disagreement must be recorded honestly.

Detailed operational procedures, conflict-resolution methods, evidence requirements, review responsibilities, enforcement mechanisms, and stage-specific pass criteria will be established in the subsequent Research Rules and Execution Protocol. Until those procedures are defined, this section establishes the governing principles and safeguards without implying that a complete enforcement system has already been implemented.

## IV. Initial Context Register

**Status:** Initial research-plan structure  
**Repository:** [C2O-UNIVERSALIS/UNIVERSALIS-OS](https://github.com/C2O-UNIVERSALIS/UNIVERSALIS-OS)  
**Scope:** UNIVERSALIS Operating System contexts only, with UNIVERSALIS Intelligence included as a shared foundational context.

This register contains six contexts: UNIVERSALIS Intelligence, the primary UNIVERSALIS Operating System context, and four associated UNIVERSALIN / UNIVERSALIN Guardian contexts. It intentionally excludes all C2O contexts and Master-system contexts. The companion UNIVERSALIS App Research Plan has its own system-specific context register and also includes UNIVERSALIS Intelligence as a shared foundation.

### I. Shared Foundational Context — 1

1. **UNIVERSALIS Intelligence**

   UNIVERSALIS Intelligence is the intelligence of UNIVERSALIS. This context researches its identity, architecture, capabilities, reasoning, learning, information processing, decision support, coordination, limitations, evaluation, and relationship to the UNIVERSALIS OS and App. It is shared by both UNIVERSALIS Research Plans and is not treated as an additional independent system or as C2O Intelligence.

### II. UNIVERSALIS Operating System Contexts — 5

1. **UNIVERSALIS Operating System**

   The operating-system context researches the UNIVERSALIS OS as an independently operable, open-source system. It examines its architecture, components, interfaces, capabilities, lifecycle, security, reliability, interoperability, governance boundaries, and relationships with the UNIVERSALIS Intelligence and associated worker and Guardian contexts.

2. **UNIVERSALIS Operating System — UNIVERSALIN**

   This context researches non-personal worker UNIVERSALINS operating in, for, or through the UNIVERSALIS OS, including their roles, capabilities, task boundaries, coordination, permissions, observability, and operational effects.

3. **UNIVERSALIS Operating System — UNIVERSALIN Intelligence**

   This context researches the intelligence, reasoning, learning, information-processing, planning, and task-performance capabilities of worker UNIVERSALINS in the UNIVERSALIS OS context, including their limitations, evaluation, and oversight.

4. **UNIVERSALIS Operating System — UNIVERSALIN Guardian**

   This context researches how human-assigned UNIVERSALIN Guardians relate to, support, and interact with humans through the UNIVERSALIS OS, preserving their personal-support role as distinct from non-personal worker UNIVERSALINS.

5. **UNIVERSALIS Operating System — UNIVERSALIN Guardian Intelligence**

   This context researches the intelligence and capabilities of UNIVERSALIN Guardians in the UNIVERSALIS OS context, including personalization, continuity, memory, human direction, privacy, consent, and the boundaries between assistance and authority.
