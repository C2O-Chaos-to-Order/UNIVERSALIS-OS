# Quantum Alchemy Taxonomy Workspace

## Purpose

This directory is the Quantum-level workspace corresponding to the source Alchemy Taxonomy of the Avatar Mind. It is reserved for examining foundational possibility, states, uncertainty, transitions, interactions, and constraints relevant to this subject area and its corresponding Ultra-level refinement.

Potential work may compare alternative state models, investigate conditions and transitions, identify interactions and dependencies, expose unresolved uncertainty, and distinguish possible configurations from supported or observed outcomes. The word "Quantum" describes this taxonomy level's conceptual and methodological scope; it does not itself establish a connection to quantum mechanics or demonstrate a physical quantum phenomenon.

Quantum-level conclusions should be traceable through the source Alchemy Taxonomy, the corresponding Meta-level examination, Super-level synthesis, Hyper-level integration, Ultra-level evaluation, and the preceding Magic-level investigation of directed transformation. They must distinguish formal models, conceptual hypotheses, empirical evidence, and verified outcomes.
