# Quantum Alchemy Taxonomy Workspace

## Purpose

This is the Quantum-level workspace corresponding to the source Alchemy Taxonomy of the Avatar Soul. It examines foundational possibility, states, uncertainty, transitions, interactions, and constraints relevant to this subject area and its corresponding Ultra-level refinement.

Potential work may compare alternative state models, investigate conditions and transitions, identify interactions and dependencies, expose unresolved uncertainty, and distinguish possible configurations from supported or observed outcomes. The word "Quantum" describes this taxonomy level's conceptual and methodological scope; it does not itself establish a connection to quantum mechanics or demonstrate a physical quantum phenomenon.

## Required Taxonomy Order

1. **Alchemy Alchemy Taxonomies**
2. **Meta Alchemy Taxonomies**
3. **Super Alchemy Taxonomies**
4. **Hyper Alchemy Taxonomies**
5. **Ultra Alchemy Taxonomies**
6. **Quantum Alchemy Taxonomies**
7. **Magic Alchemy Taxonomies**
8. **Transformative Alchemy Taxonomies**

Quantum precedes Magic. Transformative follows Magic.

Quantum-level conclusions should be traceable through the source Alchemy Taxonomy and corresponding Meta, Super, Hyper, and Ultra work, and should distinguish formal models, conceptual hypotheses, empirical evidence, and verified outcomes. Quantum-level investigation precedes Magic-level directed transformation and the final Transformative-level evaluation.
