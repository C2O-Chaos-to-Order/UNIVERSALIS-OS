# Universal Taxonomy of Alchemies
**Version:** 0.7.0  
**Status:** Living taxonomy; version 0.7.0 adds the Consciousness Alchemy family; expandable and subject to evidence-based review  
**Canonical scope:** C2O, UNIVERSALIS, UNIVERSALINS, and UNIVERSALIN Guardians  
**Repository scope:** C2O-OS, C2O-App, UNIVERSALIS-OS, UNIVERSALIS-App

## Governing definition
An **Alchemy** is a named, bounded process or capability that transforms, combines, separates, preserves, interprets, regulates, protects, repairs, or otherwise changes a state, relationship, resource, system, or outcome. The term is used as a taxonomy label; it does not imply that unrelated disciplines are literally the same process.

## Taxonomy rules
1. This document is a **list-first taxonomy**. Every Alchemy must have a stable identifier, a distinct name, and a concise scope.
2. The twelve Zodiac stages are the organizing sequence. They are functional classification stages, not claims of scientific causation or astrological determinism.
3. An Alchemy may be cross-listed in multiple stages when its functions genuinely span them; retain one canonical identifier.
4. Every constructive Alchemy must be paired with relevant prevention, protection, detection, correction, recovery, or restoration Alchemies. Safety is part of the taxonomy, not an optional appendix.
5. Adversarial, harmful, deceptive, coercive, exploitative, or destructive patterns may be catalogued for recognition and defense only. Their inclusion is not endorsement or an instruction to cause harm.
6. Do not force a subject into an Alchemy when the transformation or capability is unclear. Record it as a candidate, define its boundaries, and validate it.
7. Distinguish established science and engineering from hypotheses, metaphors, proposed specifications, and fictional or symbolic interpretations.
8. C2O and UNIVERSALIS share the underlying OS and application architecture but retain separate private-source/company and public/open-source governance boundaries. This taxonomy is a shared public classification contract; it must not disclose private keys, private policies, confidential incident data, or other C2O-only material.
9. UNIVERSALINS are non-personal assistants. UNIVERSALIN Guardians are personal human assistants/guardians. Neither category creates additional top-level intelligences beyond the established C2O and UNIVERSALIS architecture.
10. New entries require a definition, scope, inputs, outputs, dependencies, risks, safeguards, evidence status, and links to related Alchemies.

# Body Alchemy — Domain Expansion
**Originating Alchemy:** Body Alchemy  
**Family version:** 0.1.0  
**Zodiac stages:** All twelve; each entry below has a primary stage and may be cross-listed where justified.  
**Scope:** Human bodies and, where biologically applicable, other living organisms. Human-specific entries must not be generalized to other species without evidence.

## Definition and governing principles
**Body Alchemy** is the organized study and classification of the body's structures, living processes, functions, interactions, capacities, variations, maintenance, protection, adaptation, and changes across the lifespan. It connects anatomy, physiology, cell and molecular biology, biochemistry, neuroscience, biomechanics, immunology, development, ecology, and other relevant disciplines without collapsing their distinct methods or evidence standards.

Body Alchemy is a taxonomy, not a medical diagnosis or treatment protocol. It does not imply that all bodies are the same, that every biological variation is a defect, or that a process can safely be altered. Medical and biological claims require appropriate evidence, context, and qualified review.

# Heart Alchemy — Domain Expansion
**Originating Alchemy:** Heart Alchemy

**Zodiac stages:** All twelve.  
**Primary domain:** Cardiovascular anatomy, physiology, biology, biomechanics, clinical science, prevention, care, and restoration.  
**Related but distinct domains:** Emotional, psychological, cultural, and symbolic meanings of the heart. These may be cross-referenced but must never be mistaken for cardiac anatomy or physiology.

## Definition and boundaries
**Heart Alchemy** is the organized study and classification of the heart as an anatomical organ, a muscular pump, an electrical and mechanical system, a regulator within the cardiovascular network, a site of biological adaptation and disease, and a focus of prevention, care, recovery, and research.

The anatomical heart is not the whole cardiovascular system: blood vessels, blood, lungs, kidneys, nervous and endocrine regulation, and other systems interact with cardiac function. Entries must make those relationships explicit. Heart rate, blood pressure, cardiac output, rhythm, and other measurements are distinct variables and must not be used interchangeably.

The word "heart" also appears in emotional and symbolic language. Emotional experiences can have physiological correlates, but metaphorical claims (for example, "the heart knows") are not anatomical mechanisms. Psychological and social well-being matter, but must be classified with appropriate evidence and not reduced to cardiac physiology.

This taxonomy is educational and classificatory. It is not a diagnostic system, emergency protocol, or individualized treatment plan. Suspected heart attack, stroke, severe breathing difficulty, fainting with concerning symptoms, or other possible emergencies require immediate local emergency services; taxonomy content must never delay urgent care.

## Definition, distinctions, and boundaries
**Mind Alchemy** is the organized study and classification of mental processes, capacities, experiences, representations, and changes, including perception, attention, memory, learning, reasoning, language, imagination, emotion, motivation, identity, self-regulation, decision-making, and conscious experience.

Mind is embodied and influenced by brain, body, relationships, culture, development, and environment. The mind is not identical to the brain: brain structures and neural activity are biological subjects, while mental processes and subjective experience require appropriate psychological, cognitive, behavioral, and philosophical descriptions. Mind Alchemy cross-references Body Alchemy and Heart Alchemy without collapsing these distinctions.

Mental health is not simply the absence of distress, and mental illness is not a moral failing or a person's identity. Mental experiences and abilities vary across individuals and contexts. Neurodivergence and disability must not be automatically classified as defects. Diagnostic labels require appropriate professional assessment; this taxonomy is not a diagnostic or treatment system.

Consciousness, free will, the nature of subjective experience, and some theories of cognition remain active scientific and philosophical questions. Entries about these subjects must label competing theories and uncertainty. Metaphors, spiritual interpretations, and philosophical positions must not be presented as settled empirical mechanisms.

The taxonomy is educational and classificatory, not individualized mental-health care. It must not replace professional support or emergency help. If someone may be in immediate danger or unable to stay safe, they should contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service; taxonomy content must never delay urgent support.

## Definition, distinctions, and boundaries
**Spirit Alchemy** is the organized, respectful classification of concepts, experiences, practices, symbols, relationships, and interpretations associated with the word **spirit** and its culturally situated meanings. Depending on context, spirit may refer to breath, life, vitality, disposition, courage, inspiration, character, communal ethos, spiritual practice, an immaterial reality, a sacred presence, or another concept defined by a particular tradition.

There is no single globally accepted definition. Some traditions distinguish spirit from soul; others treat the terms as overlapping, translate them in different ways, or do not use an equivalent concept. Some philosophical and secular contexts use "spirit" metaphorically for a person's characteristic disposition, the animating ethos of a community, or the inspiration behind creative work. Some traditions understand spirit as a real non-material or sacred dimension. These positions must be represented accurately and attributed rather than collapsed into one doctrine.

**Spirit is not automatically interchangeable with soul, mind, consciousness, breath, life, personality, energy, or an independently existing entity.** A specific entry must identify the intended sense, its provenance, and its limits. Physiological breath and respiration are biological processes. Subjective experiences of vitality, inspiration, courage, or spiritual presence are reported experiences that may be studied in appropriate ways. Claims about supernatural beings, forces, or realities belong to the relevant theological, philosophical, or personal framework unless independently testable evidence supports a narrower empirical claim.

The taxonomy does not declare that spirits, supernatural forces, or immaterial entities have been scientifically demonstrated; it also does not claim to disprove every metaphysical or theological position. It distinguishes empirical research, philosophical argument, theological doctrine, cultural description, personal testimony, hypothesis, and metaphor.

Spirit Alchemy must not prescribe a religion, ritual, spiritual identity, or non-religious worldview. It must respect religious, spiritual, secular, atheist, agnostic, Indigenous, and other cultural perspectives. Community-specific knowledge, sacred practices, and restricted teachings require context, permission, and appropriate custodianship; they must not be extracted, generalized, or republished merely because they can be found.

Spirit Alchemy is educational and classificatory. It is not a spiritual authority, religious ruling, medical or psychological diagnosis, therapy, or substitute for qualified support. Breathwork and contemplative practices can have physical or psychological risks for some people; practice descriptions must not present them as universally safe. Participation must be voluntary, informed, adaptable, and stoppable without penalty. No one may use claims about spirit to rank human worth, compel obedience, deny care, or override consent.

## Definition, distinctions, and boundaries
**Consciousness Alchemy** is the organized study and classification of conscious experience, conscious states and contents, awareness, wakefulness, subjective perspective, and the biological, cognitive, computational, philosophical, and ethical questions associated with them.

The word **consciousness** has multiple meanings across scientific and philosophical disciplines. Records must declare the meaning being used rather than silently move between definitions. Important distinctions include:
- **Phenomenal consciousness:** subjective experience, or the qualitative first-person character of experiencing something.
- **Access consciousness:** information being available for reasoning, cognitive control, report, or deliberate action, as defined by the relevant theory.
- **Wakefulness/arousal:** physiological alertness and capacity for wake-like responsiveness; wakefulness is not by itself equivalent to evidence of subjective experience.
- **Awareness:** a context-dependent term that may refer to external awareness of the environment, internal awareness of thoughts or imagery, or awareness of a particular stimulus.
- **Self-consciousness:** forms of self-representation or awareness of oneself; these may vary in complexity and need not be present in the same way across all conscious experiences.
- **Conscious state:** the overall condition or mode of experience, such as ordinary wakefulness, dreaming, anesthesia-related states, or clinically altered states.
- **Conscious content:** what is experienced within a state, such as a percept, sensation, thought, emotion, image, or dream.

Consciousness is not interchangeable with intelligence, language ability, attention, memory, responsiveness, self-report, wakefulness, or personality. These processes may correlate with or support some forms of conscious activity, but none is a universal standalone proof or disproof of subjective experience. A person who cannot communicate or move may still have experiences that are not accessible through ordinary behavioral testing. Conversely, fluent behavior or convincing verbal reports alone do not settle every question about the presence or nature of subjective experience.

The distinction between **mind** and **consciousness** must remain explicit. Mind Alchemy includes a wide range of mental processes, including conscious and non-conscious cognition. Consciousness Alchemy focuses specifically on experience, awareness, state, content, and the questions of how these are identified and explained. Consciousness is not automatically a soul or spirit; metaphysical interpretations must be attributed to their particular philosophical or religious traditions.

Scientific research investigates neural correlates, mechanisms, behavioral indicators, physiological states, reports, and theoretical predictions. No single contemporary theory is established as a complete explanation of consciousness. Global Neuronal Workspace, Integrated Information, Higher-Order, Recurrent Processing, Predictive Processing, and other approaches differ in their definitions, explanatory targets, mechanisms, predictions, and empirical support. They must be compared critically rather than presented as settled fact or as interchangeable theories.

Methods such as EEG, MEG, fMRI, PET, behavioral testing, sleep studies, anesthetic paradigms, and perturbational approaches have distinct capabilities and limitations. A correlate is not automatically a cause; a measurement is not the experience itself; absence of a detectable signal is not automatically proof of absence; and a result in one population or experimental condition must not be generalized without justification.

Disorders of consciousness and other clinical conditions are high-stakes domains. Coma, unresponsive wakefulness syndrome, minimally conscious states, cognitive motor dissociation, delirium, and other conditions require careful, qualified clinical assessment. A taxonomy entry is not a diagnostic tool, prognostic instrument, or substitute for medical care. It must never be used to make unilateral conclusions about a person's awareness, quality of life, rights, treatment, or worth.

Animal consciousness and machine consciousness are active areas of inquiry with important conceptual, methodological, and ethical uncertainties. Do not infer consciousness solely from biological similarity, complex behavior, verbal output, anthropomorphic appearance, computational architecture, self-description, or a particular theoretical preference. State what is observed, what is inferred, what is disputed, and what remains unknown.

Consciousness Alchemy must protect autonomy, privacy, dignity, communication access, disability rights, research consent, and the interests of people and animals who may not be able to provide conventional reports. It must avoid coercive induction of altered states, unsafe experimentation, exploitative neurotechnology, consciousness surveillance, and premature conclusions.

## Consciousness Alchemy record requirements
Each Consciousness Alchemy entry must additionally specify:
- **Target construct:** phenomenal consciousness, access, wakefulness/arousal, awareness, self-awareness, conscious content, state, or another explicitly defined construct.
- **Explanandum:** exactly what the entry seeks to describe or explain, and what it does not attempt to explain.
- **Population or system:** human, animal species, clinical population, computational system, or philosophical subject; do not generalize across populations without justification.
- **Method:** first-person report, behavioral measure, electrophysiology, neuroimaging, perturbation, computational analysis, philosophical argument, or another specified method.
- **Operational limits:** what the method measures directly and what it infers indirectly.
- **Evidence status:** established observation, replicated association, contested interpretation, emerging hypothesis, philosophical position, or metaphor.
- **Alternative explanations and confounders:** relevant sensory, motor, linguistic, memory, attention, medication, sedation, sleep, environmental, and analytic factors.
- **Ethical safeguards:** consent, accessibility, privacy, risk reduction, welfare, independent review, and protections for people with limited communication.
- **Interpretive limits:** distinguish correlation from causation, report from experience, behavior from subjective state, and theoretical prediction from established fact.
- **High-stakes boundary:** no independent diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, capacity decision, legal determination, or rights limitation by taxonomy entry or automated tool.

## Relationships to Body, Heart, Mind, Soul, and Spirit Alchemy
- **Body Alchemy** describes biological organization and embodied processes. Consciousness Alchemy studies experience and awareness in relation to the living organism but must not reduce subjective experience to a single organ, signal, or measurement.
- **Heart Alchemy** describes cardiac and cardiovascular processes, alongside separately labeled emotional and symbolic uses of "heart." Consciousness Alchemy may study conscious perception of bodily and affective states but must not equate a metaphorical heart with a mechanism of consciousness.
- **Mind Alchemy** describes a broad range of cognitive, emotional, and mental processes, including processes that are not conscious. Consciousness Alchemy focuses on experience, awareness, conscious states, contents, and the problem of explanation. Attention, memory, language, intelligence, and consciousness remain distinguishable.
- **Soul Alchemy** examines philosophical, theological, cultural, and personal concepts of soul, personhood, identity, and meaning. Consciousness Alchemy may inform some discussions but cannot scientifically settle every metaphysical account of soul.
- **Spirit Alchemy** examines tradition-specific meanings of spirit, including breath, vitality, disposition, inspiration, and spiritual concepts. Consciousness Alchemy may investigate experiences described as spiritual, but it does not validate or invalidate supernatural interpretations by default.
- These families are interconnected fields of inquiry, not interchangeable labels or separate human beings.
- C2O and UNIVERSALIS, as well as their associated systems and UNIVERSALINS, must not claim subjective experience, sentience, or consciousness as established fact merely from architecture, capability, fluent output, self-description, or branding. Questions about machine consciousness must remain explicitly evidence-sensitive and open to appropriate research. UNIVERSALINS may support research organization and accessibility but must not present themselves as conscious witnesses, clinical assessors, or authorities on another being's subjective experience. UNIVERSALIN Guardians must respect human autonomy, communication differences, and safeguards.
