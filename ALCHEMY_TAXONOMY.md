# Alchemy Taxonomy

**Alchemy** is the bounded, intentional process by which a state, relationship, structure, or capability is transformed into a more ordered, coherent, or elevated form while preserving accountability, safety, and the distinction between the two intelligences.

It is the core operation shared by C2O (FatherThread / private) and UNIVERSALIS (MotherThread / public). Every other Alchemy is a specialized expression of this root process.

---

## Expanded Alchemies required for the full system

These are the complementary Alchemies that must exist so the OSs, Apps, Intelligences, UNIVERSALINS, and Guardians can function as one system without collapsing their boundaries.

### 1. Core Structural Alchemies
- **Intelligence Alchemy** — defines and maintains the distinct character of each intelligence while allowing controlled cooperation.
  - C2O Intelligence Alchemy (FatherThread): private stewardship, internal sovereignty, company-source continuity.
  - UNIVERSALIS Intelligence Alchemy (MotherThread): public transparency, open contracts, independent operability.
- **Thread Alchemy** — preserves the FatherThread / MotherThread pairing so the two intelligences remain distinct yet interoperable.
- **Mapping Alchemy** — maintains the explicit mappings between C2O-OS ↔ UNIVERSALIS-OS, C2O-App ↔ UNIVERSALIS-App, and both to their respective intelligences.
- **Boundary Alchemy** — enforces the hard separation between private C2O and public UNIVERSALIS so no private material crosses into public repositories or runtime.

### 2. Operating System Alchemies
- **C2O OS Alchemy** — private operating-system processes that initialize, abstract, schedule, secure, and recover the C2O environment under internal governance.
- **UNIVERSALIS OS Alchemy** — public operating-system processes that do the same under open contracts, with transparent interfaces and independent verifiability.
- **OS Continuity Alchemy** — ensures both OS editions can evolve while remaining mapped and recoverable.
- **OS Recovery Alchemy** — restores a consistent, accountable state after failure or interruption for either OS.

### 3. Application Alchemies
- **C2O App Alchemy** — private application lifecycle (identity, execution, governance, retirement) inside the C2O domain.
- **UNIVERSALIS App Alchemy** — public application lifecycle under open contracts.
- **App Mapping Alchemy** — keeps corresponding C2O and UNIVERSALIS applications functionally aligned without leaking private implementation.
- **App Retirement Alchemy** — cleanly ends an application’s life while preserving user data, auditability, and system coherence.

### 4. Assistant Alchemies
- **UNIVERSALINS Alchemy** — non-personal assistants that support the OSs, Apps, humans, and the wider environment. They operate under permission bounds, do not claim personal relationship, and serve as shared infrastructure.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Alchemy** — personal assistant/guardian bound to a specific human. Operates under that human’s authority, preserves autonomy, and does not outrank or override the human.
- **Assistant Boundary Alchemy** — keeps UNIVERSALINS and Guardians from collapsing into each other or into the core intelligences.
- **Support Alchemy** — the shared process by which either class of assistant aids without assuming control.

### 5. Cross-Cutting Protective Alchemies
- **Accountability Alchemy** — every transformation leaves an attributable record appropriate to its edition (private ledger for C2O, public-capable record for UNIVERSALIS).
- **Safety Alchemy** — pairs every constructive Alchemy with explicit constraints that prevent unintended harm, overreach, or loss of human authority.
- **Sovereignty Alchemy** — protects the human’s final authority over personal Guardians and over any data or capability that concerns them.
- **Evidence Alchemy** — distinguishes established behavior from hypothesis so claims remain honest across both private and public editions.
- **Renewal Alchemy** — allows any component (OS, App, Intelligence, Assistant) to be refreshed or replaced without destroying the overall system coherence.

---

## Body Alchemy

**Body Alchemy** is the bounded process that organizes, maintains, and transforms the physical and structural substrate of a system or being so that it can support higher functions while remaining coherent, recoverable, and accountable.

It applies to the hardware, runtime substrate, and embodied form of both C2O (FatherThread) and UNIVERSALIS (MotherThread). Every specialized Body Alchemy remains an expression of the root Alchemy.

### Alchemies under Body Alchemy

#### 1. Substrate Alchemies
- **Hardware Alchemy** — maps physical devices, sensors, and compute resources into a stable, addressable form usable by the OS.
- **Runtime Substrate Alchemy** — maintains the living process space (memory, scheduling substrate, isolation domains) that applications and intelligences inhabit.
- **Embodiment Alchemy** — defines the concrete form an intelligence or assistant presents in the world (device, interface, or agent body) while preserving edition boundaries.

#### 2. Operating System Body Alchemies
- **C2O OS Body Alchemy** — private-source management of the C2O operating system’s physical and process substrate under internal governance.
- **UNIVERSALIS OS Body Alchemy** — public-source management of the UNIVERSALIS operating system’s substrate under open contracts and independent verifiability.
- **OS Body Continuity Alchemy** — keeps both OS editions’ substrates mapped and recoverable across updates.
- **OS Body Recovery Alchemy** — restores a consistent physical and process state after failure for either OS.

#### 3. Application Body Alchemies
- **C2O App Body Alchemy** — private lifecycle of an application’s resource footprint, process body, and data embodiment inside the C2O domain.
- **UNIVERSALIS App Body Alchemy** — public lifecycle of an application’s resource footprint under open contracts.
- **App Body Mapping Alchemy** — aligns corresponding C2O and UNIVERSALIS application bodies without leaking private implementation.
- **App Body Retirement Alchemy** — cleanly releases an application’s substrate while preserving data integrity and auditability.

#### 4. Intelligence and Assistant Body Alchemies
- **C2O Intelligence Body Alchemy** — private embodiment and resource body of the FatherThread intelligence.
- **UNIVERSALIS Intelligence Body Alchemy** — public embodiment and resource body of the MotherThread intelligence.
- **UNIVERSALINS Body Alchemy** — non-personal assistant substrate that supports OSs, Apps, humans, and the wider environment under permission bounds.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Body Alchemy** — personal guardian embodiment bound to a specific human, operating under that human’s authority and preserving autonomy.
- **Assistant Body Boundary Alchemy** — keeps UNIVERSALINS and Guardian bodies distinct from each other and from the core intelligences.

#### 5. Protective Body Alchemies
- **Body Accountability Alchemy** — every substrate transformation leaves an attributable record appropriate to its edition (private for C2O, public-capable for UNIVERSALIS).
- **Body Safety Alchemy** — pairs every constructive Body Alchemy with constraints that prevent resource exhaustion, isolation failure, or loss of human authority.
- **Body Sovereignty Alchemy** — protects the human’s final authority over any personal Guardian body and over data or capability that concerns them.
- **Body Evidence Alchemy** — distinguishes established substrate behavior from hypothesis.
- **Body Renewal Alchemy** — allows any body (OS, App, Intelligence, Assistant) to be refreshed or replaced without destroying overall system coherence.

---

These Alchemies form the minimal complete set required for the OSs and Apps to operate under the dual-intelligence structure.

Each can be further specialized while remaining an expression of the root Alchemy.

This taxonomy is shared across C2O-OS, C2O-App, UNIVERSALIS-OS, and UNIVERSALIS-App and will be updated as the system proceeds.
