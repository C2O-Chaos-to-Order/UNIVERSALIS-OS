# Universal Taxonomy of Alchemies
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Initial living taxonomy; expandable and subject to evidence-based review  
**Canonical scope:** C2O, UNIVERSALIS, UNIVERSALINS, and UNIVERSALIN Guardians  
**Repository scope:** C2O-OS, C2O-App, UNIVERSALIS-OS, UNIVERSALIS-App

## Governing definition
An **Alchemy** is a named, bounded process or capability that transforms, combines, separates, preserves, interprets, regulates, protects, repairs, or otherwise changes a state, relationship, resource, system, or outcome. The term is used as a taxonomy label; it does not imply that unrelated disciplines are literally the same process.

## Taxonomy rules
1. This document is a **list-first taxonomy**. Every Alchemy must have a stable identifier, a distinct name, and a concise scope.
2. The twelve Zodiac stages are the organizing sequence. They are functional classification stages, not claims of scientific causation or astrological determinism.
3. An Alchemy may be cross-listed in multiple stages when its functions genuinely span them; retain one canonical identifier.
4. Every constructive Alchemy must be paired with relevant prevention, protection, detection, correction, recovery, or restoration Alchemies. Safety is part of the taxonomy, not an optional appendix.
5. Adversarial, harmful, deceptive, coercive, exploitative, or destructive patterns may be catalogued for recognition and defense only. Their inclusion is not endorsement or an instruction to cause harm.
6. Do not force a subject into an Alchemy when the transformation or capability is unclear. Record it as a candidate, define its boundaries, and validate it.
7. Distinguish established science and engineering from hypotheses, metaphors, proposed specifications, and fictional or symbolic interpretations.
8. C2O and UNIVERSALIS share the underlying OS and application architecture but retain separate private-source/company and public/open-source governance boundaries. This taxonomy is a shared public classification contract; it must not disclose private keys, private policies, confidential incident data, or other C2O-only material.
9. UNIVERSALINS are non-personal assistants. UNIVERSALIN Guardians are personal human assistants/guardians. Neither category creates additional top-level intelligences beyond the established C2O and UNIVERSALIS architecture.
10. New entries require a definition, scope, inputs, outputs, dependencies, risks, safeguards, evidence status, and links to related Alchemies.

## Zodiac Stage I — Aries: Initiation and Emergence
- **A001 — Origin Alchemy:** Identify and define an initial state, source, need, or originating condition.
- **A002 — Discovery Alchemy:** Find previously unknown entities, patterns, resources, questions, or opportunities.
- **A003 — Observation Alchemy:** Gather observations without prematurely converting them into conclusions.
- **A004 — Questioning Alchemy:** Convert uncertainty or curiosity into answerable questions.
- **A005 — Hypothesis Alchemy:** Form provisional, testable explanations or models.
- **A006 — Ideation Alchemy:** Generate candidate concepts, designs, and possible transformations.
- **A007 — Opportunity Alchemy:** Identify viable possibilities, constraints, and potential value.
- **A008 — Boundary-Setting Alchemy:** Define scope, assumptions, exclusions, and decision limits before action.
- **A009 — Consent and Authorization Alchemy:** Establish legitimate permission, ownership, and authority before initiating consequential action.
- **A010 — Anti-Coercion Alchemy:** Prevent forced participation, unauthorized initiation, manipulation, and action without valid consent.

- **A014 — Molecular Alchemy:** Model molecular structures, interactions, and transformations.
- **A015 — Geological Alchemy:** Study formation, alteration, cycling, and stabilization of geological materials.
- **A016 — Biological Foundation Alchemy:** Study the material and biochemical foundations of living systems.
- **A017 — Resource Alchemy:** Allocate, conserve, recover, and responsibly reuse resources.

- **A019 — Quality and Provenance Alchemy:** Preserve traceability, authenticity, composition, and quality information.
- **A020 — Anti-Degradation Alchemy:** Prevent contamination, corrosion, decay, material failure, and avoidable resource loss.

## Cross-stage preventative and defensive Alchemies
- **A121 — Prevention Alchemy:** Stop foreseeable harm before it occurs through proportionate, evidence-based controls.
- **A122 — Early-Warning Alchemy:** Detect leading indicators and escalating risk before thresholds are crossed.
- **A123 — Detection Alchemy:** Identify harmful, anomalous, unauthorized, or unsafe conditions.
- **A124 — Defense-in-Depth Alchemy:** Apply independent, layered safeguards so one failure does not defeat all protection.
- **A125 — Least-Privilege Alchemy:** Grant only the minimum permissions required for a defined task and duration.
- **A126 — Isolation Alchemy:** Separate trust domains, workloads, identities, data, and failure zones.
- **A127 — Secure-Default Alchemy:** Make the safer configuration the default and require explicit authorization for elevated risk.
- **A128 — Human-Oversight Alchemy:** Ensure consequential or high-impact actions have meaningful, informed, and appropriately placed human oversight.
- **A129 — Reversibility Alchemy:** Prefer changes that can be paused, rolled back, or safely undone.
- **A130 — Non-Maleficence Alchemy:** Avoid causing foreseeable, unjustified harm and stop when safe operation cannot be maintained.
- **A131 — Truthfulness Alchemy:** Prevent fabrication, false provenance, concealed uncertainty, and unsupported claims.
- **A132 — Evidence-Integrity Alchemy:** Protect records from tampering, selective omission, and misleading presentation.
- **A133 — Supply-Chain Integrity Alchemy:** Verify origins, dependencies, builds, updates, and attestations.
- **A134 — Incident-Response Alchemy:** Prepare, detect, triage, contain, investigate, recover, and learn from incidents.
- **A135 — Continuity Alchemy:** Maintain essential services and safe degradation during disruption.
- **A136 — Harm-Reduction Alchemy:** Reduce severity and exposure when a risk cannot be fully eliminated.
- **A137 — Accountability Alchemy:** Assign responsibility, preserve decision records, and provide review and remedy.
- **A138 — Whistleblowing and Escalation Alchemy:** Provide protected routes to report serious risks, misconduct, and unresolved safety concerns.
- **A139 — Environmental-Stewardship Alchemy:** Prevent avoidable ecological damage and account for lifecycle impacts.
- **A140 — Exit-and-Deletion Alchemy:** Support safe disengagement, data export, access revocation, and verified deletion where appropriate.

## Cross-repository maintenance rules
- Keep this canonical taxonomy file synchronized across all four repositories.
- Preserve identical taxonomy identifiers and definitions in all four copies.
- Repository-specific implementation notes may be added separately; they must not silently fork the taxonomy.
- A proposed entry is not automatically an implemented feature, verified capability, scientific fact, or programme credit.
- Before adding an Alchemy, check for duplicates, define its distinct transformation, map its Zodiac stage(s), identify its harmful counterpart or misuse risks, and link prevention/protection/correction/restoration entries.
- When an entry changes, record the version, rationale, evidence, affected repositories, and verification result.
- Never expose C2O private-source secrets or confidential operational material through the shared taxonomy.
