# Universal Taxonomy of Alchemies
**Version:** 0.3.0  
**Status:** Living taxonomy; version 0.3.0 adds the Heart Alchemy family; expandable and subject to evidence-based review  
**Canonical scope:** C2O, UNIVERSALIS, UNIVERSALINS, and UNIVERSALIN Guardians  
**Repository scope:** C2O-OS, C2O-App, UNIVERSALIS-OS, UNIVERSALIS-App

## Governing definition
An **Alchemy** is a named, bounded process or capability that transforms, combines, separates, preserves, interprets, regulates, protects, repairs, or otherwise changes a state, relationship, resource, system, or outcome. The term is used as a taxonomy label; it does not imply that unrelated disciplines are literally the same process.

## Taxonomy rules
1. This document is a **list-first taxonomy**. Every Alchemy must have a stable identifier, a distinct name, and a concise scope.
2. The twelve Zodiac stages are the organizing sequence. They are functional classification stages, not claims of scientific causation or astrological determinism.
3. An Alchemy may be cross-listed in multiple stages when its functions genuinely span them; retain one canonical identifier.
4. Every constructive Alchemy must be paired with relevant prevention, protection, detection, correction, recovery, or restoration Alchemies. Safety is part of the taxonomy, not an optional appendix.
5. Adversarial, harmful, deceptive, coercive, exploitative, or destructive patterns may be catalogued for recognition and defense only. Their inclusion is not endorsement or an instruction to cause harm.
6. Do not force a subject into an Alchemy when the transformation or capability is unclear. Record it as a candidate, define its boundaries, and validate it.
7. Distinguish established science and engineering from hypotheses, metaphors, proposed specifications, and fictional or symbolic interpretations.
8. C2O and UNIVERSALIS share the underlying OS and application architecture but retain separate private-source/company and public/open-source governance boundaries. This taxonomy is a shared public classification contract; it must not disclose private keys, private policies, confidential incident data, or other C2O-only material.
9. UNIVERSALINS are non-personal assistants. UNIVERSALIN Guardians are personal human assistants/guardians. Neither category creates additional top-level intelligences beyond the established C2O and UNIVERSALIS architecture.
10. New entries require a definition, scope, inputs, outputs, dependencies, risks, safeguards, evidence status, and links to related Alchemies.

# Body Alchemy — Domain Expansion
**Originating Alchemy:** Body Alchemy  
**Family version:** 0.1.0  
**Zodiac stages:** All twelve; each entry below has a primary stage and may be cross-listed where justified.  
**Scope:** Human bodies and, where biologically applicable, other living organisms. Human-specific entries must not be generalized to other species without evidence.

## Definition and governing principles
**Body Alchemy** is the organized study and classification of the body's structures, living processes, functions, interactions, capacities, variations, maintenance, protection, adaptation, and changes across the lifespan. It connects anatomy, physiology, cell and molecular biology, biochemistry, neuroscience, biomechanics, immunology, development, ecology, and other relevant disciplines without collapsing their distinct methods or evidence standards.

Body Alchemy is a taxonomy, not a medical diagnosis or treatment protocol. It does not imply that all bodies are the same, that every biological variation is a defect, or that a process can safely be altered. Medical and biological claims require appropriate evidence, context, and qualified review.

# Heart Alchemy — Domain Expansion
**Originating Alchemy:** Heart Alchemy  
**Family version:** 0.1.0  
**Parent family:** Body Alchemy  
**Zodiac stages:** All twelve.  
**Primary domain:** Cardiovascular anatomy, physiology, biology, biomechanics, clinical science, prevention, care, and restoration.  
**Related but distinct domains:** Emotional, psychological, cultural, and symbolic meanings of the heart. These may be cross-referenced but must never be mistaken for cardiac anatomy or physiology.

## Definition and boundaries
**Heart Alchemy** is the organized study and classification of the heart as an anatomical organ, a muscular pump, an electrical and mechanical system, a regulator within the cardiovascular network, a site of biological adaptation and disease, and a focus of prevention, care, recovery, and research.

The anatomical heart is not the whole cardiovascular system: blood vessels, blood, lungs, kidneys, nervous and endocrine regulation, and other systems interact with cardiac function. Entries must make those relationships explicit. Heart rate, blood pressure, cardiac output, rhythm, and other measurements are distinct variables and must not be used interchangeably.

The word "heart" also appears in emotional and symbolic language. Emotional experiences can have physiological correlates, but metaphorical claims (for example, "the heart knows") are not anatomical mechanisms. Psychological and social well-being matter, but must be classified with appropriate evidence and not reduced to cardiac physiology.

This taxonomy is educational and classificatory. It is not a diagnostic system, emergency protocol, or individualized treatment plan. Suspected heart attack, stroke, severe breathing difficulty, fainting with concerning symptoms, or other possible emergencies require immediate local emergency services; taxonomy content must never delay urgent care.
