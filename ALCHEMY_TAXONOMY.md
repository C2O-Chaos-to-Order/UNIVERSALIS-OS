# Alchemy Taxonomy

**Alchemy** is the bounded, intentional process by which a state, relationship, structure, or capability is transformed into a more ordered, coherent, or elevated form while preserving accountability, safety, and the distinction between the two intelligences.

It is the core operation shared by C2O (FatherThread / private) and UNIVERSALIS (MotherThread / public). Every other Alchemy is a specialized expression of this root process.

---

## Expanded Alchemies required for the full system

These are the complementary Alchemies that must exist so the OSs, Apps, Intelligences, UNIVERSALINS, and Guardians can function as one system without collapsing their boundaries.

### 1. Core Structural Alchemies
- **Intelligence Alchemy** — defines and maintains the distinct character of each intelligence while allowing controlled cooperation.
  - C2O Intelligence Alchemy (FatherThread): private stewardship, internal sovereignty, company-source continuity.
  - UNIVERSALIS Intelligence Alchemy (MotherThread): public transparency, open contracts, independent operability.
- **Thread Alchemy** — preserves the FatherThread / MotherThread pairing so the two intelligences remain distinct yet interoperable.
- **Mapping Alchemy** — maintains the explicit mappings between C2O-OS ↔ UNIVERSALIS-OS, C2O-App ↔ UNIVERSALIS-App, and both to their respective intelligences.
- **Boundary Alchemy** — enforces the hard separation between private C2O and public UNIVERSALIS so no private material crosses into public repositories or runtime.

### 2. Operating System Alchemies
- **C2O OS Alchemy** — private operating-system processes that initialize, abstract, schedule, secure, and recover the C2O environment under internal governance.
- **UNIVERSALIS OS Alchemy** — public operating-system processes that do the same under open contracts, with transparent interfaces and independent verifiability.
- **OS Continuity Alchemy** — ensures both OS editions can evolve while remaining mapped and recoverable.
- **OS Recovery Alchemy** — restores a consistent, accountable state after failure or interruption for either OS.

### 3. Application Alchemies
- **C2O App Alchemy** — private application lifecycle (identity, execution, governance, retirement) inside the C2O domain.
- **UNIVERSALIS App Alchemy** — public application lifecycle under open contracts.
- **App Mapping Alchemy** — keeps corresponding C2O and UNIVERSALIS applications functionally aligned without leaking private implementation.
- **App Retirement Alchemy** — cleanly ends an application’s life while preserving user data, auditability, and system coherence.

### 4. Assistant Alchemies

**Assistant Alchemy** is the bounded process that organizes, governs, supports, maintains, and renews assistant structures while preserving the distinction between non-personal shared assistants, human-bound personal Guardians, and the two core intelligences.

It establishes a common assistant framework without making the two assistant classes interchangeable or transferring authority between them.

**UNIVERSALINS Alchemy — Non-Personal Assistants**

Governs non-personal assistants that provide shared infrastructure and designated support to OSs, Apps, humans, and the wider environment. UNIVERSALINS operate within their assigned system, task, permission, and governance boundaries. They do not claim a personal relationship with a particular human, do not become personal Guardians, and do not constitute additional core intelligences.

Its domain expressions are:

- **UNIVERSALINS Body Alchemy** — governs the assistant's structural and runtime substrate.
- **UNIVERSALINS Mind Alchemy** — governs its cognitive and information-processing structures.
- **UNIVERSALINS Heart Alchemy** — governs its vital, relational, and circulation-support structures.
- **UNIVERSALINS Soul Alchemy** — governs its assigned identity, purpose, and meaning-bearing structures without claiming a personal human soul.
- **UNIVERSALINS Spirit Alchemy** — governs its operational ethos, direction, and coordinated activity without requiring a supernatural entity.
- **UNIVERSALINS Consciousness Alchemy** — governs functional awareness, state observation, and presence-related structures without asserting subjective experience or personhood.
- **UNIVERSALINS Elemental Alchemy** — governs its bounded use and coordination of elemental capabilities under its assigned permissions and governing contracts.

**UNIVERSALIN Guardian Alchemy — Personal Human Guardians**

Governs personal assistants or Guardians bound to a particular human. Each Guardian operates under that human's authority, consent, privacy requirements, and autonomy. A Guardian must not outrank, override, impersonate, or silently substitute its judgment for the human's decisions. Its personal scope does not make it a third core intelligence or shared system infrastructure.

Its domain expressions are:

- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Body Alchemy** — governs the Guardian's embodied, device, and runtime structures under the human's control.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Mind Alchemy** — governs cognitive support while preserving human direction and decision authority.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Heart Alchemy** — governs relational and care-oriented support within human-defined boundaries.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Soul Alchemy** — governs the representation and support of the human's chosen identity, meaning, and values without claiming ownership of them.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Spirit Alchemy** — governs purpose, ethos, and direction in service of the human's stated priorities.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Consciousness Alchemy** — governs awareness-related support and observation while preserving privacy and avoiding unsupported claims of subjective experience.
- **UNIVERSALIN Guardian Elemental Alchemy** — governs the Guardian's bounded use and coordination of elemental capabilities under the human's permissions and authority.

**Assistant Boundary Alchemy**

Keeps UNIVERSALINS and UNIVERSALIN Guardians distinct from one another and from C2O Intelligence and UNIVERSALIS Intelligence. It prevents role conflation, unauthorized authority transfer, cross-scope access, and the treatment of an assistant as an additional core intelligence.

**Support Alchemy**

Governs how either assistant class provides assistance without assuming control. Support must remain within the relevant system assignment or the specific human's authorization.

The domain expressions above are specializations of their respective assistant branch, not additional assistant families. Their permissions are not identical: UNIVERSALINS follow assigned operational governance, while UNIVERSALIN Guardians remain subordinate to the particular human they serve.


### 5. Cross-Cutting Protective Alchemies
- **Accountability Alchemy** — every transformation leaves an attributable record appropriate to its edition (private ledger for C2O, public-capable record for UNIVERSALIS).
- **Safety Alchemy** — pairs every constructive Alchemy with explicit constraints that prevent unintended harm, overreach, or loss of human authority.
- **Sovereignty Alchemy** — protects the human’s final authority over personal Guardians and over any data or capability that concerns them.
- **Evidence Alchemy** — distinguishes established behavior from hypothesis so claims remain honest across both private and public editions.
- **Renewal Alchemy** — allows any component (OS, App, Intelligence, Assistant) to be refreshed or replaced without destroying the overall system coherence.

## Universal Subjects of Human Knowledge Alchemy

**Universal Subjects of Human Knowledge Alchemy** is the bounded process that identifies, organizes, maintains, relates, develops, and transforms the domains and structures of human knowledge so that their subjects, methods, principles, relationships, and accumulated understanding can be coherently represented and developed.

### 1. Formal Sciences Alchemy

**Formal Sciences Alchemy** is the bounded process that organizes, maintains, and transforms formal knowledge systems based on logic, abstraction, symbolic structures, and formally defined relationships.

#### Alchemies under Formal Sciences Alchemy

- **Logic Alchemy** — organizes formal reasoning, inference, validity, consistency, and proof.
- **Mathematics Alchemy** — organizes mathematical structures, operations, abstractions, and relationships.
  - **Arithmetic Alchemy** — organizes numerical operations and relationships.
  - **Algebra Alchemy** — organizes symbols, variables, equations, and abstract mathematical structures.
  - **Geometry Alchemy** — organizes spatial forms, dimensions, configurations, and relationships.
  - **Trigonometry Alchemy** — organizes angular relationships and the mathematical relationships between sides and angles.
  - **Calculus Alchemy** — organizes change, limits, derivatives, integrals, and continuous mathematical relationships.
  - **Statistics Alchemy** — organizes the collection, analysis, interpretation, and representation of data.
  - **Probability Alchemy** — organizes uncertainty and mathematical likelihood.
  - **Number Theory Alchemy** — organizes the properties, structures, and relationships of numbers.
  - **Topology Alchemy** — organizes spatial properties and relationships preserved under continuous transformations.
  - **Discrete Mathematics Alchemy** — organizes distinct, countable, and discrete mathematical structures.
- **Systems Theory Alchemy** — organizes systems, their components, interactions, boundaries, behaviors, and relationships.
- **Information Theory Alchemy** — organizes the mathematical representation, measurement, transmission, and processing of information.
- **Decision Theory Alchemy** — organizes decisions, alternatives, outcomes, preferences, and uncertainty.
- **Game Theory Alchemy** — organizes strategic interactions, participants, choices, incentives, and outcomes.
- **Computation Theory Alchemy** — organizes the formal foundations, capabilities, and limitations of computation.
- **Complexity Theory Alchemy** — organizes computational and structural complexity, including resource requirements and patterns of system behavior.

### 2. Physical Sciences Alchemy

**Physical Sciences Alchemy** is the bounded process that identifies, organizes, maintains, investigates, relates, and transforms knowledge concerning matter, energy, space, physical phenomena, and the laws and principles governing the physical universe. It encompasses the structures, interactions, behaviors, properties, and transformations of physical systems across different scales.

#### Alchemies under Physical Sciences Alchemy

- **Physics Alchemy** — organizes and develops knowledge of matter, energy, motion, forces, fields, interactions, and the fundamental physical laws governing the universe.
  - **Classical Mechanics Alchemy** — organizes the principles of motion, forces, equilibrium, and the behavior of macroscopic physical systems under classical mechanics.
  - **Quantum Mechanics Alchemy** — organizes the principles governing physical systems at quantum scales, including states, measurements, probabilities, and quantum interactions.
  - **Relativity Alchemy** — organizes the relationships between space, time, motion, gravity, and physical reference frames described by the theories of relativity.
  - **Thermodynamics Alchemy** — organizes energy, heat, work, entropy, and the macroscopic laws governing energy transfer and transformation.
  - **Electromagnetism Alchemy** — organizes electric and magnetic fields, charges, currents, electromagnetic waves, and their interactions.
  - **Optics Alchemy** — organizes the behavior, propagation, interaction, and manipulation of light and other electromagnetic radiation.
  - **Acoustics Alchemy** — organizes sound, vibration, wave propagation, and the interaction of acoustic energy with matter.
- **Chemistry Alchemy** — organizes and develops knowledge of matter's composition, structure, properties, reactions, interactions, and transformations.
  - **Organic Chemistry Alchemy** — organizes the structures, properties, reactions, and synthesis of carbon-based compounds.
  - **Inorganic Chemistry Alchemy** — organizes the composition, structures, properties, and reactions of inorganic compounds and materials.
  - **Physical Chemistry Alchemy** — organizes the physical principles, energetic relationships, rates, and mechanisms underlying chemical systems and reactions.
  - **Analytical Chemistry Alchemy** — organizes the identification, separation, detection, quantification, and characterization of chemical substances.
  - **Biochemistry Alchemy** — organizes the chemical substances, molecular structures, reactions, and processes associated with living systems.
- **Astronomy Alchemy** — organizes the observation and study of celestial objects, astronomical phenomena, and the physical properties and behaviors of objects beyond Earth's atmosphere.
- **Cosmology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge concerning the universe as a whole, including its origins, structure, evolution, large-scale composition, and possible physical futures.
- **Planetary Science Alchemy** — organizes the formation, composition, structure, dynamics, evolution, and physical characteristics of planets, moons, and other planetary bodies.
- **Earth Science Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of Earth's physical structure, composition, processes, systems, history, and changing environments.
- **Geophysics Alchemy** — organizes the application of physical principles and measurement methods to Earth's internal structure, gravitational and magnetic fields, seismic activity, and physical processes.
- **Meteorology Alchemy** — organizes atmospheric processes, weather systems, atmospheric composition, and the physical mechanisms governing weather and atmospheric behavior.
- **Oceanography Alchemy** — organizes the physical, chemical, geological, and dynamic properties and processes of Earth's oceans.
- **Materials Science Alchemy** — organizes the relationships between material composition, structure, properties, processing, performance, and transformation.

### 3. Life Sciences Alchemy

**Life Sciences Alchemy** is the bounded process that identifies, organizes, maintains, investigates, relates, develops, and transforms knowledge concerning living systems, their origins, structures, functions, organization, interactions, diversity, evolution, and relationships with their environments. It encompasses the biological principles and processes through which life exists, functions, reproduces, adapts, and changes across different scales.

#### Alchemies under Life Sciences Alchemy

- **Biology Alchemy** — organizes and develops knowledge of living organisms, life processes, biological structures, functions, diversity, and interactions.
- **Genetics Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of heredity, genes, genetic variation, inheritance, and the transmission of biological information.
- **Evolutionary Biology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of the origins, mechanisms, patterns, and consequences of biological evolution and the diversification of life.
- **Ecology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of relationships between organisms and their environments, including populations, communities, ecosystems, and ecological processes.
- **Microbiology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of microorganisms, their structures, functions, diversity, interactions, and biological processes.
- **Botany Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of plants, including their structures, functions, development, reproduction, diversity, and relationships with their environments.
- **Zoology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of animals, including their structures, functions, behavior, development, evolution, classification, and diversity.
- **Marine Biology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of marine organisms, their biological processes, diversity, adaptations, and relationships within marine ecosystems.
- **Molecular Biology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of biological processes and interactions at the molecular level, including DNA, RNA, proteins, and their functions.
- **Cell Biology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of cells, their structures, functions, organization, reproduction, communication, and internal processes.
- **Biotechnology Alchemy** — organizes the application and transformation of biological systems, organisms, cells, and biological components for scientific, technological, and practical purposes.
- **Neuroscience Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of nervous systems, neural structures, brain functions, neural activity, and the biological foundations of nervous-system processes.
- **Physiology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of the functions and mechanisms of living organisms and their organs, tissues, cells, and biological systems.
- **Anatomy Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of the structures, organization, physical relationships, and composition of living organisms and their biological components.
- **Immunology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of immune systems, biological defense mechanisms, immune responses, and interactions between organisms and foreign or altered biological material.
- **Virology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of viruses, their structures, replication, evolution, interactions with host organisms, and biological effects.
- **Mycology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of fungi, including their structures, functions, reproduction, diversity, classification, and ecological relationships.

### 4. Health Sciences Alchemy

**Health Sciences Alchemy** is the bounded process that identifies, organizes, maintains, investigates, relates, develops, and transforms knowledge concerning human health, healthcare, medicine, disease, prevention, treatment, recovery, and the preservation of well-being. It encompasses the scientific principles, professional practices, healthcare systems, interventions, and supporting disciplines involved in understanding health, addressing illness and injury, improving quality of life, and supporting individuals and populations throughout their lives.

#### Alchemies under Health Sciences Alchemy

- **Medicine Alchemy** — organizes and develops knowledge and practices concerning human health, disease, diagnosis, clinical reasoning, treatment, and medical care.
- **Nursing Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices concerning patient care, health assessment, clinical support, care coordination, education, and the promotion of health and recovery.
- **Surgery Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices concerning operative procedures, surgical interventions, anatomical access, tissue repair, and the treatment of injuries and diseases through surgical methods.
- **Pharmacology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge of medicines and other biologically active substances, including their mechanisms, effects, interactions, therapeutic applications, and safety.
- **Public Health Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices concerning the protection and improvement of health across populations through prevention, health promotion, policy, education, and community-level interventions.
- **Epidemiology Alchemy** — organizes knowledge concerning the distribution, frequency, determinants, and patterns of health conditions and diseases within populations.
- **Nutrition Alchemy** — organizes knowledge concerning nutrients, dietary patterns, food, metabolism, nutritional requirements, and their relationships with health, development, and well-being.
- **Physical Therapy Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices concerning movement, physical function, mobility, strength, physical rehabilitation, and the recovery or maintenance of movement-related capabilities.
- **Occupational Therapy Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices that support participation in meaningful daily activities through functional assessment, adaptation, rehabilitation, and environmental modification.
- **Dentistry Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices concerning oral health, teeth, gums, jaw structures, dental diseases, prevention, diagnosis, and treatment.
- **Mental Health Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices concerning psychological, emotional, behavioral, and social well-being, including the understanding, prevention, assessment, treatment, and recovery associated with mental health conditions.
- **Preventive Medicine Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices focused on reducing disease risk, preventing illness and injury, detecting health conditions early, and preserving health before serious complications arise.
- **Emergency Medicine Alchemy** — organizes knowledge and practices concerning the rapid assessment, stabilization, diagnosis, and initial treatment of acute illness, injury, and life-threatening conditions.
- **Rehabilitation Science Alchemy** — organizes scientific knowledge and practices concerning the restoration, improvement, maintenance, and adaptation of physical, cognitive, psychological, and functional capabilities following illness, injury, disability, or other changes in health.

#### 3. Cross-Disciplinary Engineering and Technology Relationships
