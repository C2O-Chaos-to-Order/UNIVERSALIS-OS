# Ultra Alchemy Taxonomy Workspace

## Purpose

This directory is the Ultra-level workspace corresponding to the source Alchemy Taxonomy of Balance. It is reserved for evaluating and refining the integrated Hyper-level architecture in relation to this subject area. Potential work may assess resilience, adaptability, scalability, efficiency, maintainability, consistency, robustness, alternative configurations, constraints, and system-wide tradeoffs.

Ultra-level recommendations should be traceable through the source Alchemy Taxonomy, the corresponding Meta-level examination, the corresponding Super-level synthesis, and the corresponding Hyper-level integration. They must distinguish a proposed refinement from an implemented change and a tested or verified outcome.
