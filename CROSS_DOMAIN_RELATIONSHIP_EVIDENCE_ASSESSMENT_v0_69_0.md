## 1. Scope and safeguards

This assessment continues the Life Sciences ↔ Health Sciences programme using the next three pairs in the v0.68.0 regenerated remaining inventory:

- Marine Biology ↔ Health Sciences
- Molecular Biology ↔ Health Sciences
- Cell Biology ↔ Health Sciences

The assessment uses exact unordered subject-pair matching. A candidate RD-P requires more than general relevance: it must identify a contribution from each subject, a stable cross-domain research or practice boundary, and an interface that is not merely a restatement of one already-assessed specialist pair. A root-level candidate must also explain why its integrated scope adds value beyond the collection of specialist pairings and the broader Life Sciences ↔ Health Sciences relationship, without claiming that every Health Sciences activity depends on the Life Sciences subject.

The three proposed interfaces have distinct knowledge contributions and maturity profiles:

- **Marine Biology ↔ Health Sciences:** marine organismal and ecosystem knowledge contributes to cross-disciplinary health work concerning marine biological hazards, harmful algal blooms and seafood safety, aquatic food resources, marine biodiversity and bioresource discovery. The common boundary is marine biological evidence entering health-related surveillance, exposure assessment, food-safety and nutrition work, and selected biomedical discovery. The existence of marine-derived bioactivity is not proof of clinical efficacy; many candidates remain preclinical, and sustainable sourcing and translation remain constraints.
- **Molecular Biology ↔ Health Sciences:** molecular mechanisms and methods contribute to research and health systems through nucleic-acid and protein analysis, gene expression and regulation, molecular markers, molecular diagnostics, multi-omic investigation and selected precision-health pathways. This is a cross-cutting mechanism-and-measurement interface rather than a claim that molecular evidence is appropriate for every health decision. Analytical validity, causal interpretation, clinical utility, equitable access and implementation must be assessed for each application.
- **Cell Biology ↔ Health Sciences:** cellular structure, function, interactions, signalling, differentiation, repair and cell-based models contribute to disease research, biomarker development, tissue and organoid models, regenerative research and selected cell-based therapies. The boundary is cellular-level evidence and models entering health research and defined translational pathways. Organoids and cell products do not reproduce the full complexity of a human organism, and product-specific clinical use requires rigorous quality, potency, safety and efficacy evidence.

A candidate RD-P recommendation is a proposal for owner review, not an accepted or registered canonical edge. It does not establish hierarchy, dependency, enablement, implementation, testing, verification or owner acceptance. No canonical change is made by this report.
