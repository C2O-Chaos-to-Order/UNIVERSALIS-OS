# Super Alchemy Taxonomies

## Purpose

This collection provides higher-order workspaces for synthesizing and examining patterns across the 33 corresponding Alchemy Taxonomy workspaces. Its focus is cross-taxonomy structure: recurring principles, shared distinctions, higher-order relationships, dependencies, correspondences, coherence, and integration across subject areas.

The Super level is intended to build carefully supported syntheses from source taxonomies and related Meta-level examination. It is not simply a duplicate of the source taxonomies or a replacement for their individual subject scopes.

## Relationship Between the Taxonomy Levels

- **Alchemy Taxonomies** provide the subject- and domain-focused source workspaces.
- **Meta Alchemy Taxonomies** examine the corresponding source taxonomies as objects of study, including their scope, structure, definitions, relationships, methods, and evidence.
- **Super Alchemy Taxonomies** examine higher-order patterns and syntheses across the source taxonomies, including how distinct subject areas may relate or integrate.
- **Hyper Alchemy Taxonomies** examine system-wide coherence, architecture, interactions, constraints, and integration across the Super-level syntheses.

These levels are complementary. A higher-level synthesis does not automatically supersede, validate, or alter a source taxonomy or a Meta-level finding.

## Status

All 33 Super Alchemy Taxonomy workspace directories are being initialized as organizational structures. Their Super-level research artifacts have not yet been populated, assessed, reviewed, or adopted. No higher-order synthesis, relationship, or principle is established merely by creating this structure.

## Boundary

This structure does not itself create, alter, validate, approve, or canonize any source taxonomy entry, definition, relationship, hierarchy, classification, governance rule, or owner decision. Super-level findings must preserve provenance and distinguish proposed syntheses, supporting evidence, evidence status, review status, owner acceptance, and authorization. Conflicting or unresolved source evidence must remain visible rather than being silently harmonized.
