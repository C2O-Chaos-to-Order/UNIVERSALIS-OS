## 1. Scope and safeguards

This report continues the Life Sciences ↔ Health Sciences cross-domain screening programme. The selected subgroup examines three interfaces: comparative animal biology in transplant surgery; anatomical body composition and regional adiposity in preventive risk assessment; and cellular changes in preventive cancer screening.

The assessment distinguishes three scopes:

- **Zoology ↔ Surgery:** animal species, comparative organ anatomy, and species-specific biological characteristics in translational surgical research, experimental transplantation, and xenotransplantation. The evidence is strongest for porcine models and early clinical pig-organ transplantation. This does not mean that Zoology is a routine prerequisite for all surgery, that animal models are identical to human anatomy, or that xenotransplantation has established long-term efficacy.
- **Anatomy ↔ Preventive Medicine:** body structure, regional adipose compartments, anthropometric landmarks, and anatomical distribution of adiposity in the assessment of selected obesity-related and cardiometabolic risks. The interface is limited to structural phenotyping and measurement. It is distinct from the physiological mechanisms and clinical measurements assessed in Physiology ↔ Preventive Medicine (LSHS-185).
- **Cell Biology ↔ Preventive Medicine:** cellular morphology, differentiation, and early neoplastic changes as the scientific basis for selected cytology-based preventive screening, particularly cervical cytology. The applied screening pathway is delivered through clinical cytopathology and preventive-care systems; the evidence does not make all cell biology a preventive service. Primary high-risk HPV screening is preferred for many average-risk adults aged 30–65 under updated guidance, with cytology retained in defined circumstances.

A candidate RD-P recommendation is a proposal for owner review, not an accepted or registered canonical edge. A candidate does not establish hierarchy, dependency, enablement, implementation, testing, or verification. No canonical change is made by this report.
