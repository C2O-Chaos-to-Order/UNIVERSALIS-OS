## 2. Method and decision rule

Each relationship is evaluated at the exact canonical subject-pair level. Evidence for a narrow application is not treated as proof that either complete discipline is contained within, dependent on, or equivalent to the other.

The report distinguishes:
- a disciplinary or practice interface from shared identity, equivalence, or hierarchy;
- application evidence from evidence of a general whole-subject relationship;
- knowledge contribution from operational dependency or enablement;
- evidence strength from owner acceptance and canonical registration.

A candidate RD-P recommendation is an evidence-supported proposal for owner review. It is not an accepted edge, a canonical registration, or proof of implementation, testing, or verification. Relationship direction is recorded only where a meaningful contribution is supported; otherwise it is left non-directional or reciprocal. Evidence labels such as “High” and “Moderate” are qualitative first-pass judgments, not calibrated or standardized evidence grades; this assessment does not define a scoring rubric.
