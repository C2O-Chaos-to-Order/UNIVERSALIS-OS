## 2. Assessment method and decision rule

Each proposed connection is assessed at the exact canonical subject-pair level. The review distinguishes:
- a recognised research or practice interface from identity, equivalence, or containment;
- evidence of a bounded subdomain interface from evidence for a relationship across the full scope of either subject;
- knowledge contribution from dependency, enablement, or operational necessity;
- evidence quality from owner acceptance and canonical registration.

A candidate RD-P recommendation means that the sources justify a bounded relationship proposal for owner review. It does not mean that the edge has been accepted, added to the canonical taxonomy, implemented, tested, or verified. Direction is recorded only where the evidence supports a meaningful contribution; otherwise the interface is reciprocal or direction is not asserted. Qualitative labels such as “High” are provisional evidence-fit judgments, not calibrated evidence grades; this assessment does not define a scoring rubric.

## 4. Reassessment of previously deferred pairs

These are reassessments of previously counted unique pairs, not additional unique pairs.

### LSHS-008 — USA-LS-010 Molecular Biology ↔ USA-HS-005 Pharmacology

**Previous disposition:** Not promoted because co-inclusion in an integrated medical curriculum did not establish a pair-specific relationship.

**New evidence [S77]:** The NIH Intramural Research Program explicitly defines molecular pharmacology as the study of molecular actions and interactions of drugs or therapies, describes its role in translational discovery and validation of therapies, and identifies molecular biologists and pharmacologists among the contributing disciplines.

**Revised disposition:** Candidate RD-P, with scope restricted to molecular pharmacology, drug-target interactions, and the molecular basis of therapeutic action. This reassessment does not imply that the full disciplines are equivalent, nested, or universally dependent.

### LSHS-016 — USA-LS-011 Cell Biology ↔ USA-HS-004 Surgery

**Previous disposition:** Not promoted because the earlier evidence did not establish a sufficiently bounded direct relationship across the subject pair.

**New evidence [S83, S84]:** Clinical material on Mohs micrographic surgery describes wound healing as essential to surgical outcomes and details the cellular and molecular events involved. A 2024 review in *Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology* describes coordinated cellular and molecular mechanisms of skin wound healing.

**Revised disposition:** Candidate RD-P, strictly limited to the surgical wound-healing, tissue-repair, and regenerative-surgery interface. This is not evidence that Surgery as a whole is a branch of Cell Biology, or that all surgical practice depends on cell-biology research.
