# Taxonomy of Alchemy

## Status

New research programme. Foundational scope only; research findings and taxonomy classifications are to be developed and verified.

## Purpose

Establish the foundational taxonomy of Alchemy for the C2O and UNIVERSALIS ecosystems.

## Research foundations

This work will independently research:

1. The historical Magnum Opus and its documented variations.
2. The twelve zodiac signs and historically attested correspondences.
3. The Emerald Tablet, including its textual history, translations, and interpretations.
4. The relationship among these bodies of material, distinguishing historical evidence from proposed synthesis.

## Research integrity

- This is new work.
- Do not import, reuse, reconstruct, or rely on previous Human Tablet work or superseded Alchemy Taxonomy material.
- Do not assume that one historical alchemical sequence represents all alchemical traditions.
- Distinguish primary-source evidence, scholarly interpretation, historical claims, and proposed C2O/UNIVERSALIS classifications.
- Record sources, editions, translations, methods, uncertainties, and unresolved questions.
- Treat zodiacal correspondences as research questions until supported and explicitly classified.
- Do not present symbolic or philosophical interpretations as scientific findings.

## Initial organization

Research materials may be organized into the following areas as evidence and deliverables are developed:

- Foundations and Principles
- Magnum Opus
- Alchemical Operations
- Alchemical Stages and Transformations
- Zodiacal Correspondences
- Emerald Tablet
- Integrated Alchemical Framework
- Sources and Historical Evidence

These are organizational headings, not claims that historical alchemy had one universally accepted structure.

## Repository scope

This foundational taxonomy is maintained consistently across the C2O and UNIVERSALIS OS and App repositories and their corresponding private implementation repositories, subject to each repository's governance and publication boundaries.
