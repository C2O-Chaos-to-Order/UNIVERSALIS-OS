# Meta Alchemy Taxonomy of Abjuration

## Purpose

This workspace is the Meta-level counterpart to the [Alchemy Taxonomy of Abjuration](../../Alchemy%20Taxonomies/Alchemy%20Taxonomy%20of%20Abjuration/). It is reserved for examining that source taxonomy as an object of study: its scope, terminology, definitions, structure, classifications, relationships, dependencies, methods, evidence, consistency, context, and governance boundaries.

## Meta Research Scope

Meta-level research may examine:

- The meaning, scope, and boundaries of the source taxonomy of Abjuration.
- Definitions, terminology, distinctions, categories, and classification criteria.
- The structure and internal organization of the source taxonomy.
- Relationships, dependencies, correspondences, overlaps, and contradictions.
- The provenance, context, reliability, and limitations of source materials.
- Methodological consistency, version history, unresolved questions, and evidence gaps.
- How claims and classifications should be reviewed, maintained, and governed.

Where the subject has historical, cultural, religious, philosophical, literary, game-related, or other specialized uses, preserve the provenance and distinguish the contexts. Do not conflate fictional or symbolic descriptions with historical or empirical claims.

## Relationship to the Source Taxonomy

This workspace examines and documents the corresponding Alchemy source taxonomy; it does not silently replace, rewrite, expand, validate, or canonize that source. Meta observations, questions, and proposals must remain distinguishable from accepted source definitions or classifications.

## Research Record

For each investigation, preserve the research question, scope, sources, methods, assumptions, inputs, outputs, limitations, unresolved questions, and version history. Distinguish proposed observations, documented claims, tested findings, verified conclusions, review status, owner acceptance, and authorization.

## Status and Governance Boundary

Workspace initialized. Meta-level research artifacts have not yet been populated, assessed, reviewed, verified, or adopted. No definitions, relationships, classifications, or canonical decisions are established merely by creating this directory. Changes to the shared Alchemy Taxonomy require explicit owner acceptance and authorization.
