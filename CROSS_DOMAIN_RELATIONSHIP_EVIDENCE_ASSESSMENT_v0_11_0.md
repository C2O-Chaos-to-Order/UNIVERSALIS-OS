## 2. Method and decision rule

Each pair is assessed for evidence of a specific interface between the two canonical subject scopes. Evidence that animals, plants, or ecological conditions can affect health is not sufficient on its own; the relationship must be tied to an identifiable clinical, nursing, or preventive practice.

The review distinguishes:
- a relationship between distinct canonical subjects from shared identity or equivalence;
- a bounded clinical, nursing, or preventive interface from a whole-subject relationship;
- knowledge contribution from dependency or enablement;
- evidence quality from owner acceptance and canonical registration.

A candidate RD-P recommendation is a proposal for owner review, not an accepted or registered canonical edge. Direction is recorded only where the sources support a meaningful contribution or information flow.

## 4. Duplicate and scope cross-check

The seven pairs were checked against assessment snapshots v0.1.0 through v0.10.0. None is an exact duplicate of a previously screened unordered pair.

Two pairs in the proposed 3 × 3 grid were excluded because they already exist:
- Zoology ↔ Preventive Medicine — LSHS-057, previously assessed in v0.9.0;
- Ecology ↔ Preventive Medicine — LSHS-059, previously assessed in v0.9.0.

Related but distinct pairs that must not be conflated:
- Zoology ↔ Emergency Medicine (LSHS-068) is an acute-care animal-injury and zoonotic-exposure interface. Zoology ↔ Preventive Medicine (LSHS-057) concerns animal-associated exposure prevention more broadly.
- Zoology ↔ Nursing (LSHS-069) is limited to nursing care after animal bites and related exposures, and is distinct from nursing's broader infection-control relationships.
- Botany ↔ Emergency Medicine (LSHS-070) concerns botanical toxicant identification in acute poisoning assessment. Botany ↔ Preventive Medicine (LSHS-072) concerns prevention of plant-related occupational and individual exposures.
- Botany ↔ Nursing (LSHS-071) is screened but not promoted because the current evidence does not establish a sufficiently specific botanical nursing scope.
- Ecology ↔ Nursing (LSHS-074) concerns professional nursing engagement with environmental/ecological determinants, not the previously screened Ecology ↔ Public Health (LSHS-007) or Ecology ↔ Preventive Medicine (LSHS-059).
- Ecology ↔ Emergency Medicine (LSHS-073) is not promoted because environmental emergencies do not, without a clearer ecological-science contribution, establish a subject-pair relationship.
