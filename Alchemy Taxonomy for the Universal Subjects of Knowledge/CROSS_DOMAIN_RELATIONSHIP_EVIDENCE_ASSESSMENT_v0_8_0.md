## 2. Method and decision rule

Each pair is assessed for evidence of a specific interface between the two canonical subject scopes. Evidence for a focused application is not treated as proof of equivalence, containment, or universal dependency.

The review distinguishes:
- a relationship between distinct canonical subjects from identity or equivalence;
- bounded clinical, research, or preventive interfaces from whole-subject relationships;
- knowledge contribution from dependency or enablement;
- evidence quality from owner acceptance and canonical registration.

A candidate RD-P recommendation is a proposal for owner review, not an accepted or registered canonical edge. Direction is stated only where a meaningful contribution or information flow is supported. Every relationship below is bounded to the scope described in its row.

## 4. Duplicate and scope cross-check

The seven pairs were compared against the subject-pair rows in assessment snapshots v0.1.0 through v0.7.0. None is an exact duplicate of a previously screened unordered pair.

Nearby relationships must remain distinct:
- LSHS-012, Mycology ↔ Medicine, is not the same pair as Mycology ↔ Dentistry, Emergency Medicine, Nursing, or Preventive Medicine. The new records address different clinical and professional contexts.
- LSHS-013, Microbiology ↔ Nursing, is not the same pair as Mycology ↔ Nursing. The new pair is bounded to fungal infection and fungal pathogen precautions, not the full microbiological domain.
- LSHS-014, Microbiology ↔ Surgery, is not the same pair as Microbiology ↔ Dentistry. The new pair concerns oral microbial communities, biofilms, and dental disease rather than perioperative infection.
- LSHS-035 and LSHS-046 already cover Immunology ↔ Emergency Medicine and Immunology ↔ Preventive Medicine, respectively. The present Immunology ↔ Dentistry and Immunology ↔ Nursing pairs are different unordered subject pairs.
- LSHS-040 through LSHS-047 include several infectious-disease and infection-control connections, but none is an exact duplicate of the seven pairs screened here.
